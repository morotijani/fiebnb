Trust & safety

A stay you can walk into with confidence

Every listing on Fiebnb passes through real checks before it goes live — not just a photo upload. Here's what stands behind that.

What we check

Built-in protections for every booking

ID-verified owners

Every host — hotel, guesthouse or private landlord — submits a national ID or business registration before listing.

Site inspections

New listings are visited or photo-verified by our local team so what you see is what you get on arrival.

Secure, escrowed payments

Money is held by Fiebnb, not the host, and only released 24 hours after a smooth check-in.

Two-way reviews

Guests and hosts both leave reviews after every stay, so patterns of bad behaviour surface quickly on either side.

Local support, real hours

A Ghana-based team on call by phone and WhatsApp — not a chatbot reading from a script.

Simple reporting

Report a listing, a guest or a host directly from any booking. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Behind every listing

Verification isn't a one-time checkbox

Hosts are re-verified when they add new properties, and listings with recurring guest complaints are paused automatically while our team investigates. For long-term rentals, we also confirm proof of ownership or management authority — so you're never paying a deposit to someone with no real claim to the property.

Something feel off?

Report it directly from your booking, or reach our safety team any time.
