About us

Built in Accra, for how Ghana actually finds a place to stay

Fiebnb started with a simple complaint: booking a hotel abroad was easier online than booking one three neighbourhoods away. We set out to fix that.

Our story

From a WhatsApp group to a national platform

Fiebnb began in 2023 as a WhatsApp group connecting travellers with vetted guesthouses in Accra. As demand grew from Kumasi, Takoradi and Cape Coast, the group turned into a platform — and property owners started asking for the same trust and reach for their long-term rentals, not just nightly stays. Today Fiebnb covers all three: hotels, guesthouses, and long-term apartments and homes, in one place.

What we believe

The principles behind the product

01

Trust is earned, not assumed

Every host is checked before they're listed. We'd rather have fewer listings than unreliable ones.

02

Ghana isn't a niche market

We build for Mobile Money, local addresses, and the way people actually search — not a global template with our logo on it.

03

Hosts are our other customer

A guesthouse owner in Takoradi deserves the same quality tools as a hotel chain in Accra.

04

A stay is more than a transaction

Fie means home. We try to make every booking feel a little closer to that, whether it's for one night or one year.
