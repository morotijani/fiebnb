For property owners

Fiebnb has your property's back

Hosting strangers — or tenants — shouldn't mean carrying all the risk yourself. Here's what's covered when you list on Fiebnb.

Verified guests only

Every guest confirms their identity before booking, so you always know who's arriving at your door.

Damage protection

Accidental damage during a stay is covered up to ₵50,000 for short stays, with claims reviewed within 5 business days.

Guaranteed payouts

Once a guest checks in, your payout is locked in — even if a dispute is raised afterward, you're paid on schedule.

Dispute support

Our team mediates guest disputes directly so you're not left negotiating refunds or damages on your own.

Long-term tenant screening

For rentals, we run basic reference and ID checks on applicants before they can send a booking request.

Priority host line

A dedicated host support number, separate from guest support, for issues that need a faster response.
