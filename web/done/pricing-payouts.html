Common questions

Pricing, answered

Is there a fee to list my property?

No. Creating and publishing a listing is free, for both short-stay and long-term properties. You're only charged once a booking is completed.

When exactly do I get paid?

For short stays, payout is released 24 hours after the guest checks in. For long-term rentals, each month's rent is released 3 days after the guest's payment clears, giving time to resolve any issues.

Can I set my own prices?

Yes, fully. You control nightly or monthly rates, minimum stay lengths, and seasonal pricing from your host dashboard.

What if a guest cancels — do I still get paid?

It depends on your cancellation policy and how close to check-in the cancellation happens. See Cancellation options for the exact breakdown.
