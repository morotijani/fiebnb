Guest guide

Cancellation options

Every listing carries one of these policies, shown clearly on the listing page before you book. Long-term rentals follow separate notice-based terms.

Short-stay policies

Compare the three policy types

Long-term rentals

Notice periods, not nightly refunds

Before move-in

Cancel any time before your move-in date for a full refund of your deposit, minus the Fiebnb service fee.

After move-in

Standard notice is 30 days in writing through the platform. Rent already paid for the current period is non-refundable.

Deposit handling

Deposits are held by Fiebnb, not the landlord, and returned within 7 days of move-out minus any documented damages.

Special cases

When the usual rules don't apply

What if the host cancels on me?

You get a full refund, no matter which policy applied, plus help from our team finding a comparable stay — often at no extra cost.

Do extenuating circumstances get special treatment?

Serious illness, bereavement, or travel disruption documented with proof may qualify for a full refund outside the standard policy — contact support to review your case.

Can I change my dates instead of cancelling?

Yes, most hosts allow a free date change if the new dates are available — message them directly from your booking before cancelling.
