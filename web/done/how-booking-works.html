Guest guide

How booking on Fiebnb works

Four steps stand between you and a place to stay in Ghana — whether that's one night in Osu or six months in East Legon.

01

Search & compare

Filter by city, stay length, property type and price to see verified hotels, guesthouses and rentals side by side.

02

Request or book instantly

Short stays with "Instant Book" confirm right away. Long-term rentals go to the owner as a request, usually answered within a day.

03

Pay securely

Pay by Mobile Money or card. Funds are held by Fiebnb and only released to the host 24 hours after you check in.

04

Check in & settle in

Get check-in details, host contact and directions in the app. Message your host any time before or during your stay.

Short stays vs. long-term

Booking looks a little different depending on how long you're staying

For nightly stays at hotels and guesthouses, most bookings confirm instantly and payment covers the full stay upfront. For long-term rentals, owners typically ask a few questions first, request the initial month plus a refundable deposit, and may ask for reference checks — much like renting anywhere else, just without the agent fees.

Common questions

Booking, answered

Do I need to pay the full amount upfront?

For short stays, yes — the full stay total is charged when your booking is confirmed. For long-term rentals, you'll typically pay the first month and a deposit to secure the place, then pay monthly after that.

What's the difference between "Instant Book" and "Request to book"?

Instant Book listings confirm the moment you pay — no waiting on the host. Request to book listings ask the host to review your dates or application first, which is common for long-term rentals and some guesthouses.

Can I message the host before booking?

Yes. Every listing page has a message option so you can ask about parking, water supply, generator backup or anything else before you commit.

What happens if my host cancels on me?

You're refunded in full and our support team helps you find a comparable stay — see Cancellation options for details.
